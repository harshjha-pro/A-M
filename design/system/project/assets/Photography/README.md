# Photography

Warm wedding-mood placeholders: marigold garlands, candlelight, champagne drapes, rose petals, lehenga and sherwani fabrics, a feast table and stage florals. They show where real photographs go.

- `hero-golden-hour` — Home cover. In the app this slot shows the couple's own cover photos from Settings › Home photos (up to 8).
- `engagement-blush`, `mehndi-garden`, `champagne-drapes`, `night-lights`, `marigold-phere`, `reception-candlelight` — event and workspace covers.
- `lehenga-burgundy`, `sherwani-ivory`, `rose-petals`, `stage-florals`, `feast-table` — moodboards, decor and dish thumbnails.

Always put white text on these over the `scrim`. Swap in real photos by uploading them here and pointing `AM.PHOTOS` at the new files.
